(13 s) The phones swap short chirps. Arrival times and echoes give acoustic evidence, checked on each phone. The proof shows both phones signed the same session, without revealing what went in. What the circuit states today (circuits/copresence.circom): attestationHash = Poseidon(timestamp, nonceA, nonceB, audioKeyHash); both EdDSA signatures over it verify; nullifier = Poseidon(attestationHash, pubKeyA, pubKeyB). Public: attestationHash, nullifier. Everything else private. Outside the circuit: the acoustic checks themselves (ranging, NEAR/FAR verdict). The circuit can't tell a real recording from an edited one; trust in the phone app carries that. Don't say "raw audio stays on the device" or claim replay/relay resistance on stage until the data flow confirms it.
