# sound-bound handoff, 2026-09-23

Fresh start for acoustic co-presence. Replaces the proximity-echo direction. Read this first, then README.md.

## Why we restarted

The proximity-echo work measured distance to the centimeter with strict gates (0.15 ms clock residual, extra-chirp audit). It reached 0.01 ms timing on clean runs and could see 5 cm vs 25 cm move, but kept withholding results on side effects: a faint recurrent tail 160 to 200 ms after each chirp, 20 ms zero-filled holes in browser recordings, and 0.25 ms peak-lobe flips from a narrow 2 to 6 kHz chirp. The last state of that work is in `../proximity-echo/docs/2026-09-23-late-tail-and-timeline.md`. Both policies added there are opt-in offline and the live server there is unchanged.

The reframing, agreed with the user:

- A proof needs a bound, not a ruler. The one thing an absent party cannot do is make a sound arrive earlier than physics allows, when it could not know the sound in advance.
- Adversary model: a stranger between two honest phones. A dishonest phone cannot be caught acoustically in a browser, because honest audio latency and a network relay both sit at 100 to 300 ms. This is a protocol demo under a trusted-client assumption. NFC's real advantage is a secure element, not 4 cm.
- Four-arrival timing stays. It cancels play latency and clock offset. Precision needed is about 1 ms, not 0.15.
- Each phone plays its own random wideband code. Kills the self/partner aliasing that broke the earlier echo-compare work, and gives entropy against pre-play.
- Room-echo spectrum compare (the Proximity-Echo paper's mechanism) is dropped. It proves same room, not distance. Echo loudness ratio (direct to reverb) is kept as a free second distance cue.
- Target: separate 30 cm or less from 1 m or more. Touch detection is a later question and needs the wideband body-echo experiment nobody has done.

What this establishes:

- The four-arrival ruler tracks distance on real devices. Touch reads 2 cm, 1 m reads 94, 1.5 m reads 155. Rounds within a session agree to a centimeter or two. 30 cm and 100 cm are separated by about 65 cm of margin. The bias at close range is small and roughly plus 5 to 10 cm, not the 13 cm seen with the old narrow chirp.
- DRR falls monotonically with distance: about 7 dB at touch, 5 at 5 cm, 2 at 30 cm, minus 7 at 1 m. It agrees with the ruler in every session. Nine dB between 30 and 100 cm.
- Echo cancellation off was honored on Mac Chrome and Android Chrome. iPhone has not been tried.

Two rough edges found:

1. The Android 20 ms hole is still present (2 of 8 sessions, always exactly 960 zero samples). When it falls between two of a round's four arrivals, only that round shifts by 20 ms and reads about minus 330 cm. The other rounds are unaffected because a shift before both arrivals cancels in the four-arrival difference. The current rule only discards a round whose search window overlaps the hole. Fix: discard any round where a flat run lies between the earliest and latest expected arrival in that recording. Both UNDECIDED sessions become NEAR with that rule and three usable rounds.
2. Code cross-fire margin is 7 to 10 dB in the room versus 19 dB in the clean model. Reverb smears the correct code's energy across the window. The 100 cm session hit 6.7 dB against a 6 dB reject line. Fix: lengthen the probe from 30 to 60 ms in `probes.py` for about 3 dB, and if that is not enough, move to a code family designed for low cross-correlation.

Minor: the Mac clipped 2247 samples at touch, harmless here. Every recording reports exactly one worklet discontinuity, which looks like an accounting artifact at the trim boundary rather than a real gap; confirm so it cannot mask a real one.

## What is not verified

iPhone has not been tried. Only one room, one device pair. The DRR line between NEAR and FAR has not been set; it is reported, not gated. Nothing is signed yet.

Chrome automation on the Mac could not click through the self-signed cert interstitial, so browser testing was done by the user on devices, not by automation.
