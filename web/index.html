      What a count means

      Our PoP server attests that two app-signed, enrolled app instances, each bound to that ENS name, measured each other acoustically at under about 60 cm during hour T, and both owners agreed to publish it.

      met counts distinct names met; meetings counts every recorded meeting; zk counts meetings whose proof our server checked offchain. Everything on this page is read straight from Ethereum Sepolia (ENSv2 beta) through the Universal Resolver, with no backend. Published meetings are permanent and public: both names and the hour. ENSv2 Sepolia is a beta that ENS resets from time to time.
